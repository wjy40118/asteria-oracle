8. Avoid fixed language such as "destined," "guaranteed," "certain" or "doomed."

10. Keep the response concise, clear and emotionally grounded.

2. Describe both individual processes before the interaction between them.
3. Explain which components form the resonance score.
4. Never describe the score as a probability of relationship success.
5. Do not guess the other person's private thoughts or decide separation, marriage or reconciliation for either person.
